stupidbot Commands List
========================

Anyone can say these in chat:
- "Meow, tp to me."          -> bot sends /tpa VOlcarona_Alt
- "Meow, tp me to you."      -> bot sends /tpahere VOlcarona_Alt

VOlcarona_Alt or SpeedStrafe04 only:
- "Meow, enable totem mode."   -> bot crouches 3 times, equips a Totem of
                                   Undying to its offhand (or says "Totem
                                   required" if it has none), and
                                   auto-re-equips a new totem whenever one
                                   is consumed
- "Meow, disable totem mode."  -> bot says "Totem mode disabled." and stops
                                   auto-re-equipping totems
- "Meow, do <command>"         -> bot runs /<command>
                                   (type the command without a leading slash,
                                   e.g. "Meow, do tpa SpeedStrafe04")
- "Meow, drop item."           -> bot drops whatever it's currently holding
- "Meow, offhand."             -> bot swaps whatever it's currently holding
                                   into its offhand

Automatic (no chat trigger needed):
- Picking up any armor piece (helmet/chestplate/leggings/boots) auto-equips
  it, replacing whatever's currently worn in that slot, and drops the old
  piece on the ground. Only works if the picked-up armor lands in the
  hotbar (a server limitation, not a choice).
